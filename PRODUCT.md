## Platform

web

## Users

O1 Media serves a two-sided marketplace with distinct jobs-to-be-done:

1. **Businesses & Local Brands (Hospitality, Dining, Retail, D2C):**
   - **Situation:** Seeking predictable local footfall, customer acquisition, and measurable conversion from social attention rather than vanity metrics.
   - **Job-to-be-done:** Coordinate vetted creator activations, launch localized tasting/footfall campaigns, and capture inbound traffic on high-converting websites with ongoing maintenance.

2. **High-Impact Creators & Influencers (Rising 5k+ to Established 500k+):**
   - **Situation:** Spending excessive time on manual editing and inbound negotiations while leaving money on the table with weak rate cards.
   - **Job-to-be-done:** Secure long-term tier-one brand partnerships, outsource short-form video editing to a dedicated desk, and establish a professional web media kit with zero upfront cost.

## Product Purpose

O1 Media bridges creator influence directly to commerce, localized footfall, and digital conversion architecture. Success means:
- For Brands: Measurable revenue lift, lines out the door for restaurant openings, and transparent ROAS.
- For Creators: Defended rate cards, recurring brand sponsorships, and hours saved on video production.

## Positioning

Full-funnel integration under one roof:
Neighboring influencer agencies only broker contacts and walk away, while digital agencies only build websites. O1 Media operates the complete unified pipeline:
`Vetted Creator Matchmaking → Viral 9:16 Video Production → Conversion-Engineered Web Pages → Footfall & Revenue Attribution`.

## Operating Context

- **Geography:** Global remote operations anchored by hyper-local activations in major metro hubs (London, NYC).
- **Channels & Formats:** 9:16 short-form video (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts), paired with custom landing pages and digital care plans.
- **Decision Duality:** The entire digital experience enforces a dual-funnel architectural fork (Brands vs Creators) across headers, interactive calculators, case studies, and intake forms.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Confirmed Capabilities:**
  - Interactive ROI and creator rate card simulator with dynamic range sliders.
  - Interactive 9:16 vertical phone mockup reel player with niche filter tabs (`all`, `hospitality`, `editing`, `web`).
  - Dual persona view switching synchronized across header, cards, calculator, and contact forms.
  - Dark luxury aesthetic (`#06080C`, accent `#F4BD45`, white text `#FFFFFF`) with adaptive light mode (`#F8FAFC`, amber-gold `#C27E00`).
  - Lenis smooth scroll foundation synchronized with GSAP ScrollTrigger blur reveals.
- **Technical Stack:**
  - React 18, TypeScript, Vite 6, Tailwind CSS 3, GSAP 3 + ScrollTrigger, Lenis, Lucide React.
- **Constraints:**
  - Zero tolerance for clipped text, misaligned button icons, or low-contrast light/dark mode collisions.
  - Strictly $\le 2$-line hero headlines across all viewports.

## Product Principles

1. **Duality in Every Decision Point:** Never force a brand or creator to guess their path; provide explicit, reactive dual funnels at every major transition.
2. **Attention Without Conversion is Wasted Capital:** Viral views must terminate in a localized queue or a high-converting web landing page.
3. **Outcome-Driven Transparency:** Quantify every deliverable with verified metrics (retention, views, ROAS, footfall lift).
4. **Frictionless Agency Intake:** 24-hour SLA response for brand discovery and creator representation inquiries.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Maintain WCAG AA compliant contrast ratios across both Dark Mode (`#0C1014`) and Light Mode (`#f5f7fa`).
- Ensure all interactive controls have accessible semantic roles (`role="radiogroup"`, `role="tab"`, `aria-selected`, `aria-checked`).
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` across all GSAP timelines and Lenis smooth scrolling.
